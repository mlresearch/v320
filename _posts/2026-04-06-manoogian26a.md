title: Unifying neural population dynamics, manifold geometry, and circuit structure
abstract: A central aim of neuroscience is to understand how the dynamics of neural
  circuits give rise to cognitive functions such as perception, attention, and decision-making.
  Cortical neural circuits are hierarchical and recurrent, resulting in rich temporal
  dynamics of individual neurons and distributed selectivity across the population.
  Classical neural circuit models capable of characterizing cognitive processes struggle
  to account for this complexity of cortical responses. Recent approaches leveraging
  heterogeneous neural networks address this complexity by characterizing activity
  in terms of interactions among latent states. In these lecture notes, we highlight
  recent work aimed at increasing the interpretability of these models and relating
  them to classical circuit models. These new analytical approaches connect neural
  population dynamics, geometry of latent manifolds, and the underlying circuit structure
  to enable mechanistic insights into cognitive processes.
